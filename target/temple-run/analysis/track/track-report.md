# Temple Run APK — track asset research

Package `com.imangi.templerun` 1.39.0, Unity 2022.3.62f2, source `/Users/yuminghao/Downloads/temple-run.xapk`.
All 160 Mesh / 147 MeshRenderer / 55 Material / 168 Texture2D objects in every `.assets` file were
inventoried (see `apk-inventory.json`); the 120 environment meshes were rendered to
`contact-sheet-0..3.png` and `contact-sheet-top-1..2.png`.

## Headline finding

**There is no road tile, no junction and no modular track kit in the APK.**
The 120 environment meshes are pieces of one hand-built static temple diorama (`Object01–Object282`):
decorated crenellated walls, gates/arches, rock outcrops, tree trunks. There is no Terrain/TerrainData
object anywhere, and no plain floor slab of any size — the endless road surface must be generated by
game code at runtime. What *is* shipped, and what is genuinely reusable, is the **wall set**: one family
of 60-unit crenellated wall modules (with L-corner and gate variants) that can dress a procedurally
generated road.

## Usable now for an endless runner

Verified assembly (`preview.html?cam=run|iso|top`, screenshots `preview-corridor-run.png`,
`preview-corridor-iso.png`, `preview-corridor-top.png`): a 30 u wide corridor, walls every 60.17 u:

- left wall: `rotation.y = Math.PI`, `x = -(halfWidth + 11.36)`
- right wall: `rotation.y = 0`, `x = +(halfWidth + 11.36)`
- `y = 0`, `z = tile centre`; gate centred on the corridor at the same x/rotation.

From the chase camera it reads as Temple Run: stone courses, gold trim band, crenellated parapet.
Scale check: the rigged `player` mesh is 10.8 u tall, so 1 u ≈ 0.18 m — walls are ~3.4 character
heights tall, the 30 u corridor ~2.8 character heights wide.

Not available: any floor/road tile, any T-junction or fork, any rail/edge pieces, any purely
zero-thickness wall (all candidates are 24–40 u deep shells).

## Caveats (important)

1. **The pieces are 2.5D stage-set facades, not solid modular geometry.** Wireframes
   (`wire-straight-wall.png`, `wire-corner.png`) show one large decorated wall plane plus a fake
   perspective "batter" of a few huge triangles converging to a vanishing point; the pieces are hollow
   behind and read correctly only from the decorated side. Render them `THREE.DoubleSide` and always
   keep the decorated face toward the track; the fake depth then hides itself.
2. **Winding/normals are inconsistent in the source** (some faces inverted). Do not trust exported
   normals for lighting direction; `DoubleSide` + a fill light from the opposite side of the sun
   removes the black facets (this is what the preview does).
3. **Chirality of the corner.** The L wraps two adjacent edges of its 50×50 box, so the four 90°
   rotations give all four turn directions but only the *concave* arrangement. A convex (outside) wrap
   would need a mirrored copy — not exported, since mirroring an atlas-mapped facade flips its texture.
4. **Gaps at seams.** Pieces were hand-placed in the original level and their fake-perspective braces
   leave visible gaps/slivers when tiled exactly; overlap them by ~0.5 u and let the wall body hide it.
5. **No junctions.** If the runner needs T-splits or forks, the only original option is the gate
   (`track-gate-a.obj`) as a portal; anything else must be built.

## Recommended next step

Keep procedural road generation and use these OBJ as a *dressing layer*: spawn one wall pair per
60.17 u tile at the verified offsets, a gate every N tiles, a corner wall pair on bends, and scatter
rock/tree behind the walls to hide the hollow backs and the horizon. If a true modular road surface is
needed, it must be authored (or ripped from the game's runtime geometry), not extracted from this APK.
